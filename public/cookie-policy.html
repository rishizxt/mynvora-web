Cookie Policy

What we store, why, and how to turn it off.

Mynvora uses a small number of cookies and local storage items. Here's exactly what they do.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience. Mynvora also uses related technologies like local storage, session storage, and mobile device identifiers. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

CategoryPurposeExamplesOpt-out
Essential Required for the Service to function — login, security, session management. Cannot be disabled. Authentication tokens, session ID, CSRF token, load balancing Not possible
Preference Remember your settings and choices across sessions. Discovery filters, language, notification settings, onboarding state Settings → Privacy
Analytics Help us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Page views, feature clicks, error logs, crash reports Settings → Privacy
Marketing Used to show sponsored ad cards from partners. We do not share your email, phone, or precise location. Ad impression counters, ad preference signals Settings → Privacy

3. First-party vs third-party cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by Mynvora on your device when you use our Service. Third-party cookies are set by our service providers — for example, Cloudinary (photos), Razorpay (payments), and Google (Sign-In). We do not allow third-party advertising networks to place tracking cookies on Mynvora.

4. Session vs persistent cookies

Session cookies expire when you close your browser or app. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a specified period (typically 30 days to 2 years) or until you delete them.

5. How to manage cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Privacy to turn analytics and marketing cookies on or off.
  • Browser: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent Mynvora from working.
  • Device: Your operating system may have an option to limit ad tracking.

6. Mobile app

The Mynvora mobile app does not use tracking cookies or SDKs from third-party advertising networks. It uses the platform's native secure storage for authentication tokens and preferences.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Mynvora currently does not respond to DNT signals, but you can control analytics cookies via the in-app settings.

8. India-specific

For users in India, our cookie practices comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Personal data collected through cookies is processed only with your consent, for the purposes stated above. You may withdraw consent at any time through Settings.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Continued use of the Service after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
