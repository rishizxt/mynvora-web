Privacy Policy

We don't sell your data. Ever.

Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how to get rid of it. Plain language, not legalese.

Mynvora ("we", "us", "our") operates the Mynvora mobile application and website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

We wrote this policy to match the transparency standards set by leading platforms in our category. If anything is unclear, contact us at mynvora@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Mynvora is a dating and social discovery platform for verified people aged 16 and over. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), Mynvora is the data controller (or "Data Fiduciary" under the DPDP Act) of personal data processed through the Service.

Contact: mynvora@gmail.com

3. Special category data DPDP Act 2023

Face geometry and sexual orientation are considered special categories of personal data under GDPR and India's DPDP Act. We process them only with your explicit consent, given when you:

  • Complete selfie verification (face geometry), or
  • Choose to add your sexual orientation to your profile.

You may withdraw consent at any time by removing the relevant feature from your profile or by contacting mynvora@gmail.com.

6. Automated decision-making and profiling

Mynvora uses automated systems to:

  • Order your discovery feed — based on your filters, activity, and location.
  • Moderate content — flag photos that may violate our Terms.
  • Detect fraud and abuse — rate-limit spam accounts and suspicious login patterns.

You have the right to request human review of any automated decision that significantly affects you (such as an account suspension). Contact mynvora@gmail.com.

7. Cookies and tracking

The Mynvora web app uses the following storage mechanisms:

  • Essential storage — authentication tokens, session data. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference storage — your discovery filters, notification preferences, onboarding state. Stored locally on your device.
  • Analytics (web only) — aggregate page views and error logs. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy.

The Mynvora mobile app does not use tracking cookies or SDKs from third-party advertising networks. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected:

  • Active accounts: retained while your account is open.
  • Deleted accounts: profile, photos, messages, matches, and swipes are anonymized or deleted immediately. Transaction records are retained for 7 years in anonymized form to comply with tax and financial law.
  • Session logs: up to 12 months.
  • Support requests: up to 3 years.
  • Moderation records: up to 5 years (to detect repeat offenders).

9. Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS 1.2+.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds).
  • Refresh tokens are hashed and stored server-side.
  • Database access is restricted to a limited set of service credentials.
  • Photos are served over signed CDN URLs.
  • We conduct periodic audits of access and admin actions.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password and enable device lock. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact mynvora@gmail.com immediately.

11. International transfers

Mynvora's servers are located in the United States and Europe, provided by Render, Neon, Cloudinary, and Vercel. If you access the Service from outside these regions, your data will be transferred to and processed in them.

For transfers from the EU/UK, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, plus additional technical and organisational measures.

For transfers of personal data from India, we comply with the DPDP Act, 2023 and any rules made thereunder, ensuring an adequate level of protection.

12. Children and age limits

Mynvora is intended for users aged 16 and over. Users aged 16–17 have restricted features and cannot interact with accounts marked 18+.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we learn that a user is under 16, we will remove the account and delete associated data. If you believe a minor is using Mynvora, please report it to mynvora@gmail.com.

Under India's DPDP Act, 2023, processing the personal data of children requires verifiable parental consent. Mynvora does not direct any part of the Service to children under 16.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you in-app and by email at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.

Continued use of the Service after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
