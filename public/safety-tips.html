Safety Tips

Your safety comes first. Always.

Dating online should feel exciting — not risky. These are the practical steps we recommend, the tools we built to protect you, and the helplines to turn to if something goes wrong.

Safety tools built into Mynvora

Photo Verification

Verified users get a blue checkmark on their profile. Look for it before you match. Verification uses a live selfie that we compare against your profile photos — no filters, no fake accounts.

AI Photo Moderation

Every photo you upload is scanned for nudity, violence, and prohibited content by Sightengine before it goes live. This happens automatically — usually within seconds.

Block & Report

Block anyone, any time, from any screen — their profile, your match list, or mid-conversation. Blocked users cannot see you or contact you. Reports are confidential and reviewed by a human.

Approximate Location Only

We show nearby people, but never your exact GPS coordinates. Distances are rounded and displayed as approximations (e.g., "5 km away"). You can disable location in Settings.

Incognito Browsing

Available on Diamond. Your profile is only visible to people you've already liked. Nobody else sees you in their discovery feed.

Mutual Match Only

No one can message you unless you've both liked each other first. This means zero unsolicited messages from strangers.

Meeting in person — best practices

Video call before you meet

A 5-minute video call confirms they're the person in their photos. It's the single most effective way to avoid catfishing.

Public place, daytime, your own transport

First dates should be somewhere you know — a café, restaurant, park. Go there yourself and leave yourself. Don't get in their car and don't share a ride home.

Tell a friend where you'll be

Share their name, a screenshot of their profile, the venue, and a check-in time. If you don't check in, your friend knows to act.

Watch your drink

Never leave your drink unattended. If you feel unusually drowsy or unwell, call a friend or emergency services immediately.

Never send money

No matter the story — emergency, hospital bill, customs hold, crypto investment — if someone you met online asks for money, it's a scam. Report them.

Don't share Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details

No genuine date needs your Aadhaar number. These are the same data points scammers use to open fake accounts in your name.

Trust your gut

If something feels off — the story doesn't add up, they push for your address, they rush intimacy — leave. You don't owe anyone politeness at the cost of your safety.

How to report someone

Report from their profile

  • Open the person's profile
  • Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner
  • Tap Report
  • Select the reason and add any details you're comfortable sharing

Report from your match list or chat

  • Open the chat with the person you'd like to report
  • Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of the chat
  • Tap Report
  • Select the reason and add details

Report by email

If you can't report in-app, email us at mynvora@gmail.com and include:

  • The reason for your report
  • The exact name, age, bio, and photos on the profile (screenshots are best)
  • Any location, phone number, or email address you have for the person

What happens after you report

  • All reports are treated as confidential.
  • Our Trust & Safety team reviews every report.
  • The reported user may receive a generic notification that their account was reviewed, but the reporter's identity is never disclosed.
  • Exceptions may be made in rare instances where required by law.
  • Submitting false or malicious reports can harm others and may result in enforcement against the reporter.

Red flags to watch for

They claim to be overseas

Military, oil rig, doctor abroad — a classic romance-scam setup.

They rush to move off Mynvora

WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal "because it's easier" — a sign they want the conversation off our radar.

They refuse video calls

Or keep rescheduling them. Always a red flag.

They ask for Aadhaar, PAN, UPI, or OTP

"To verify your identity." Genuine matches never ask.

They send crypto or forex links

Investment scams targeting dating-app users are common. Report immediately.

They ask for intimate photos, then threaten

This is sextortion. Report to cybercrime.gov.in and to us at mynvora@gmail.com. Do not pay.

Their photos look too polished

Reverse-search them on Google Images. Catfish often use stolen photos.

Enforcing our policies

What we do when rules are broken

When we identify a violation of our Community Guidelines, our Trust & Safety team takes action to address it. This may include:

Warning

A warning that the behavior is not allowed. Multiple warnings can lead to suspension or ban.

Content Action

Removing the violating content or hiding it from others' view.

Verification

Requiring automated checkpoints like a captcha, photo, or age verification.

Suspension

Suspending a user's access to Mynvora for a period of time.

Ban

Banning a user's profile from Mynvora and/or other affiliated platforms.

Law Enforcement

In situations involving immediate danger, working with authorities. Child abuse material is reported to the relevant national agency and/or local law enforcement.

We don't believe all violations are the same and regularly evaluate whether the resulting action is appropriate for the behavior. We also work with Indian cybercrime authorities when a report involves a criminal offence.
