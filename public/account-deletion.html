Account Deletion

Leaving? We'll make it easy.

Delete your account in-app or by email. We'll never make you jump through hoops.

Note for Indian users: If you are unable to delete your account via the app or email, you may file a grievance with our Grievance Officer at mynvora.app/grievance. Requests are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Mynvora app on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Send an email to mynvora@gmail.com

Include:

  • The email address or phone number associated with your Mynvora account
  • Send from the same email address you registered with (so we can verify it's you)

We process emailed deletion requests within 7 days. You'll receive a confirmation once the account is deleted.

What gets deleted

Profile

Your profile, photos, and bio are removed immediately.

Matches

Your matches and conversations are permanently deleted.

Swipes

All your likes and passes are removed.

Subscription

Any active subscription is cancelled — no refund.

What we keep

  • Anonymized transaction records — kept for up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance. Required by law. No personal data attached.
  • Aggregate analytics — anonymized metrics with no identifiers, used to understand app performance.

India-specific rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — request a copy
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Grievance redressal — file a complaint with our Data Protection Officer

To exercise any of these, email mynvora@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Can I recover my account?

No. Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your profile, matches, messages, and photos cannot be recovered. If you just need a break, consider deactivating instead — deactivation hides your profile but keeps your data intact for when you return.

Need help?

If you have trouble deleting your account, or if you believe your account was deleted by someone else, contact us immediately at mynvora@gmail.com.
