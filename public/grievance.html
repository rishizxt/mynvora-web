Grievance Redressal

Complaints acknowledged in 24 hours.

In accordance with India's Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, we provide a grievance redressal mechanism for all users in India.

If you are in immediate danger, call 112 (emergency) or 181 (Women's Helpline). This page is for complaints about our platform, not emergency services.

What we can help with

  • Content takedown requests (harassment, impersonation, non-consensual imagery)
  • Account access issues
  • Impersonation or fake profile reports
  • Data privacy and DPDP Act requests (access, correction, erasure)
  • Payment and subscription complaints
  • Abuse of the reporting system
  • Any other complaint regarding our compliance with the IT Act, 2000 and IT Rules, 2021

How to file a grievance

  1. Email mynvora@gmail.com with the subject line "Grievance — [Your account email]".
  2. Include your registered email or phone number, a clear description of the issue, and any supporting evidence (screenshots, URLs, profile IDs).
  3. You will receive an acknowledgment within 24 hours.
  4. We will investigate and provide a resolution within 15 days.

Escalation — Grievance Appellate Committee

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may appeal to the Government of India's Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC) within 30 days of receiving our decision. The GAC is accessible at gac.gov.in.

Law enforcement requests

Lawful requests from law enforcement, courts, or government authorities should be sent to mynvora@gmail.com with the subject line "Law Enforcement Request". We respond to valid legal process in accordance with applicable law.

Data Protection Officer (DPO)

For requests under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (access, correction, erasure, grievance), you may contact our Data Protection Officer at mynvora@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
