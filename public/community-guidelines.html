Community Guidelines

Real people. Real rules. Real respect.

Every account on Mynvora is one human being — with a story, boundaries, and the same right to safety that you have. These aren't suggestions. They're the floor we all stand on.

The Rules
01

Respect boundaries

Comfort levels vary. What feels fine to you might cross a line for someone else. Read the signals, ask before assuming, never push past a "no."

  • No nudity, sexual content, or sexual solicitation on public profiles.
  • Consent matters in every conversation — not just physical ones.
  • If someone unmatched or stopped replying, let it go. That was their answer.
02

Share carefully

Your personal information is yours. Don't broadcast it, and don't ask others to hand theirs over.

  • No phone numbers, emails, home addresses, or social handles in your public bio.
  • Never share bank details, UPI IDs, OTPs, or passwords — no matter the story.
  • If someone you've never met asks for money, it's a scam. Report it.
03

No violent content

Mynvora is a positive space. We don't allow content that glorifies harm.

  • No gore, death imagery, or descriptions of violence against humans or animals.
  • No weapons displayed in a threatening context.
  • No content advocating self-harm or eating disorders.
04

Personal connections, not business ones

Mynvora is for meeting people — not for growing your Instagram, selling courses, or promoting an MLM.

  • No advertising, fundraising, campaigning, or selling products.
  • No sharing social handles for followers.
  • No sex work, escort services, or compensated relationships.
  • No religious or political solicitation.
05

Just be you No fake profiles

People want to meet the real you. Not a persona, not a filtered version, not someone else entirely.

  • No fake accounts, catfishing, or impersonation — even "for fun."
  • Use photos of yourself, taken recently. AI face replacements are not allowed.
  • Impersonation is a criminal offence under Section 66D of India's IT Act, 2000.
  • Use the first name you actually go by. We don't require legal names — we require honesty.
06

Communicate respectfully

Not every conversation will click — that's fine. Letting it become someone else's problem is not.

  • No harassment, threats, bullying, doxing, sextortion, or blackmail.
  • No racism, casteism, communalism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or ableism.
  • If someone doesn't fit your preference, unmatch quietly.
  • Pause before you react. Some messages can't be unsent.
07

Kindness over harm

Actions that intend or cause harm — on the app or after you've met — will be taken extremely seriously.

  • Anything calling for or inciting harm against a person or group is banned.
  • If someone on Mynvora has hurt you, take care of yourself first. Then report, if you can.
  • We work with Indian cybercrime authorities when a report involves a criminal offence.
08

Adults only 16+ with age tiers

Mynvora requires users to be at least 16. Users aged 16–17 have a restricted tier and can only interact with other 16–17 accounts. Hookups mode and certain features are 18+ only.

  • No photos of minors — not even of yourself as a child. This is a hard rule.
  • No images of unaccompanied minors or minors who are unclothed.
  • If we learn a user is under 16, the account is removed immediately.
  • Under India's DPDP Act 2023, children's data requires verifiable parental consent.
09

Abide by the law IT Rules 2021

No illegal content or activity. Ever.

  • No drugs, counterfeit goods, or stolen items.
  • No human trafficking, sexual exploitation, or child abuse material — full stop.
  • No content violating India's IT Act 2000, IT Rules 2021, or DPDP Act 2023.
  • We remove unlawful content within 3 hours of a government order, and 2 hours for non-consensual intimate imagery.
10

One person, one account

Each account belongs to one person. Not a couple, not two friends, not you and your alter ego.

  • Don't share your login with anyone.
  • Don't create a second account after being banned.
11

Post your own content Copyright Act 1957

You own what you post. So does everyone else.

  • Don't post photos or private messages from others without consent.
  • Don't upload copyrighted or trademarked work that isn't yours.
  • Sharing intimate images without consent is a criminal offence under Section 66E of India's IT Act.
12

Be a good Mynvora user

Don't abuse the platform or the people on it.

  • Don't spread false or misleading information.
  • Don't spam harmful links or unsolicited content.
  • Don't mass-create accounts.
  • Don't scam people into sending money or items.
  • Don't submit false or malicious reports — this harms real people.
  • Don't use third-party apps or bots to unlock features.
13

Stay active, or we'll tidy up

If you don't log in to Mynvora for two years, we'll assume the account isn't in use and may delete it. Come back every so often so you're seen.

India-Specific
IN

Legal age, consent, identity

  • Marriage Act: Legal marriage age is 18 for women, 21 for men. We do not facilitate or encourage child marriage.
  • Sections 354A–D, IPC: Sexual harassment, assault, stalking, and voyeurism are criminal offences. We cooperate fully with law enforcement.
  • Section 67, IT Act: Publishing obscene material electronically is punishable with imprisonment. We remove such content proactively.
  • Section 66D, IT Act: Impersonation using a computer resource is punishable. No fake profiles.
  • Grievance Officer: Per IT Rules 2021, contact mynvora@gmail.com. Complaints acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 15 days.
  • Grievance Appellate Committee: Appeal to the Government's GAC at gac.gov.in.
IN

Data protection under DPDP Act 2023

  • We collect only the personal data we need, and only with your consent.
  • You can access, correct, and erase your data any time from Settings.
  • You can nominate another person to exercise your data rights on your behalf.
  • Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days.
IN

Indian cybercrime reporting

  • If you are the victim of online fraud, sextortion, or harassment, report it to India's National Cyber Crime Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
  • Mynvora will cooperate with any lawful investigation, including preserving records for the period required by law.
  • For women in distress: dial 181 (Women's Helpline). For general emergencies: dial 112.
Enforcement

What happens if you break the rules

We don't ban people on a whim — but when we act, we act fairly. Our enforcement is proportional to what you did.

Nudge

A gentle reminder in-app for a minor or unclear violation.

Warning

A formal warning. Repeat violations escalate.

Feature Restriction

Temporary loss of specific features (messaging, hookups, etc.).

Ban

Permanent removal for serious or repeated violations, without refund.

Off-platform behaviour can also lead to removal. If you harm someone you met on Mynvora, that's a Mynvora matter.

Appeals

If you believe action was taken in error

Email mynvora@gmail.com with your account email and a description of what you think was mistaken. A human reviewer will look at your case.
